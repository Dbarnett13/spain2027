## Priority for the two goals

Salesforce partnering: Agentforce World Tour Barcelona (June 2027), then Agentforce World Tour Madrid (April 2027), dreamOle 2027, DES Malaga (June 2027), eShow Barcelona (April 2027), The MarTech Summit Madrid (May 2027).

Corporate AI strategy and responsible AI: AI Congress Barcelona (October 2026), Gartner IT Symposium (November 2026, expensive), AI Summit Barcelona (September 2027), MWC 2027, AI Tech Summit Barcelona (April 2027).

## Verified negatives

- No Barcelona edition: AI and Big Data Expo (Amsterdam, London), Web Summit or Collision, OMR (Hamburg), Forrester (Amsterdam, London), Gartner Marketing or Data summits (London), IDC Spain (Madrid).
- No Anthropic, OpenAI, or NVIDIA own events in Barcelona. Their staff speak at AI Summit Barcelona. OpenAI's first Spanish office opens in Madrid in H2 2026.
- Microsoft AI Tour, Google Cloud AI Live, and AWS Summit are Madrid only.
- The AI Conf Barcelona (2026-11-03 to 11-04) is cancelled.
- AESIA (Spanish AI supervision agency) runs no standalone conference. Its director appears at DES and Data for Policy. Data for Policy 2026 was 09-08 to 09-10 at UPF Barcelona, 2027 host not announced.
- Excluded as pay-to-present conference mills: AIML 2027, AGSAIML 2027, Explainable Ethical and Responsible AI 2027.
