# Salesforce partner campaign, Barcelona, October 2026

Purpose: meet every Salesforce partner with a Barcelona presence during the 10-day Barcelona leg of the October 2026 trip, to open partnering conversations (overflow, specialist, white-label, referral) for after the May 2027 move. Target list and tracker live in plan/outreach/salesforce-partners.md. Added 2026-09-11.

Constraint: work for Spanish entities counts against the 20 percent cap on Spanish-source revenue for a self-employed DNV holder. Frame every conversation as capacity partnership, not employment. See plan/workstreams/income-continuity.md.

## Offer (what you are bringing to the table)

- Senior Marketing Cloud, Marketing Cloud Personalization, and Data Cloud delivery capacity, US-enterprise references, available in CET hours from May 2027.
- Overflow and specialist staffing on their projects, white-label under their brand, or referral of their teams into US opportunities.
- Superstoke remains a US entity. Spanish revenue stays under 20 percent by design, so the ask is a small number of engagements, not a full book.

## Channels, in order of use

1. Named contact by email (Salesforce practice lead, alliances lead, or Spain managing director). Best response rate.
2. LinkedIn connection request with a note, then InMail or message once connected. Use for firms where no email is found.
3. Warm intro via a Salesforce partner account manager (PAM) in Spain or a US Salesforce contact. Ask one US Salesforce AE or PAM for intros to the Spain partner team before sending cold emails.
4. Contact form or generic info@ address. Last resort, always paired with a LinkedIn touch to a named person.
5. In person at community events during the trip (see Events below).

## Meeting plan (30 minutes)

1. Two minutes: who you are, why Barcelona, that Superstoke stays a US entity.
2. Ten minutes: their Salesforce practice. Headcount, MC and Data Cloud share of work, how they staff peaks, use of freelancers or subcontractors, biggest delivery gaps, Agentforce demand.
3. Ten minutes: fit. Overflow, specialist (MCP, Data Cloud, Journey architecture), white-label, referrals into US deals.
4. Five minutes: commercial shape and next step. Day rate range, subcontract framework, NDA, who owns the relationship.
5. Ask for one referral: another firm or person in Barcelona you should meet.

Bring: one-page capability sheet (English and Spanish), rate card, two anonymized US case studies, business cards.

## Questions to answer in every meeting

- How is Marketing Cloud and Data Cloud demand trending in Spain for 2027?
- How do they subcontract today: freelancer platforms, direct autonomos, other agencies?
- Do they contract with non-Spanish entities, and any issue invoicing a US LLC?
- Who in the firm approves subcontractors?
- Which clients or verticals are driving Agentforce and Data Cloud work?
