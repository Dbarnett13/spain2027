## Partner program route (FY27 rules, effective March 2026)

- Tiers collapsed to Select and Summit. New partners enter as Provisional. 28 competencies scored on certifications, completed projects, and CSAT.
- Steps: sign up at partners.salesforce.com/pdx/s/signup, apply at salesforce.com/partners/become-a-partner, complete the due diligence questionnaire and Entity at a Glance packet, Partner Operations and Legal review, activated as Provisional, complete the Accelerate for Consulting Partners course and business plan, then a Partner Advisor contacts you. A named PAM typically arrives at Select or with revenue.
- Select needs roughly 2 certifications plus 1 validated project, minimum 1 employee with 2 certs or 2 employees with 1 each. Annual fee not found in accessible sources.
- If Superstoke already holds a US partner account, it is global. Ask the current US Partner Advisor or PAM for an intro to the Iberia Alliances team instead of reapplying.
- No public Salesforce Spain alliances email or form found. Route is Partner Community plus direct LinkedIn to the priority 1 names.
