## Question lists

Universities
- Is the fall 2027 intake confirmed? When is the decision made and when is the application deadline?
- Is the degree official or titulo propio? Total tuition and payment schedule?
- Exact weekly schedule and in-person days. Any coding or data-science modules?
- Do they admit senior professionals? Any recommendation letter or interview?
- Do they help with the visa or NIE? Does the international office have vetted lawyer referrals?

Kindergartens and schools
- Can a child arriving in May 2027 enter that school year? Process and documents for out-of-period placement.
- Languages of instruction. Fees. Waitlist length for September 2027.
- Hours, after-school care, and location relative to candidate neighborhoods.

Relocation agencies and lawyers
- How many DNV cases filed through the UGE, and approval rate?
- Do they handle the LLC administrator structure for Beckham?
- Flat fee in writing including requerimiento responses? Family dependents in the same filing?
- Housing search: how they get landlords to accept a foreigner without a Spanish payslip.

Neighborhood walk
- Rent for a 3-bedroom, noise, parks, dog areas, transit time to the university and to the kindergarten, grocery and pharmacy proximity.

### Housing rules to know before viewing apartments

- Landlords ask for passport, NIE if held, income about 3 times rent, payslips or contract, bank reserves of 2,000 to 4,000 EUR. Without a Spanish payslip: bank guarantee of 6 to 12 months, a Spanish guarantor, or a guarantee company.
- Deposit: one month fianza plus at most two months guarantee, three months total, by law.
- Agency fees for habitual-residence leases are paid by the landlord in Catalonia.
- Barcelona is a tense rental zone: new contracts are capped by the reference index or the prior rent. Llei 11/2026 in force July 14, 2026.
- Seasonal rentals (1 to 3 months) are regulated by Llei 11/2025 since January 1, 2026: purpose must be stated in the contract, same rent caps apply. Listed on Idealista (alquiler temporal), Flatio, aTemporal, HousingAnywhere, Ukio, Blueground, Spotahome.
