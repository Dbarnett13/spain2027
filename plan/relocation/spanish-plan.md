# Spanish to B1 by May 2027

**Window:** August 2026 to May 2027 (9 months)
**Load:** 10 hrs/week, roughly 400 total hours
**Target:** Solid B1, edging into B2 in listening and reading
**Advantage applied:** Italian background compresses the A1 to A2 stage significantly

## Daily Template

The plan assumes most audio is stacked onto things you already do. Only the evening block requires sitting down.

| Slot | Time | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | 15 min | Anki reps, first thing, non negotiable |
| Stacked audio | 30 min | Language Transfer or podcast during dog walk, cooking, commute |
| Evening block | 45 min | Focused study, tutor session, or reading |
| **Weekday total** | **1.5 hrs** | |

**Weekend adjustment**

- Saturday: 2 hrs, one long tutor session plus a full episode of TV with Spanish subtitles
- Sunday: 1 hr, reading and Anki catch up, no new material

**Weekly total: 9.5 to 10.5 hrs**

## Phase 1: Foundation
**August to October 2026 | ~120 hrs**

Goal: internalize the grammar system and stop translating from English. Your Italian does most of the heavy lifting here, so this phase moves fast.

**Core**

- **Language Transfer, Complete Spanish.** Work through all 90 tracks. One to two tracks per day during stacked audio. This is the single highest leverage thing in the whole plan and it is free.
- **Dreaming Spanish, Superbeginner and Beginner.** Start at 20 min/day, build to 30. Filter for Spain based creators.
- **Anki.** Spanish frequency deck, top 2000 words. 15 new cards/day. Sentence cards, not isolated words.

**Speaking**

- Start tutoring in week 3, not week 1. Get the Language Transfer grammar under you first.
- 2 x 60 min/week on italki. One structured tutor, one conversation only tutor. Both from Spain.

**Milestone by end of October**

- All 90 Language Transfer tracks complete
- ~1200 words in active recall
- Can hold a 20 min tutor conversation on familiar topics with heavy tutor support

## Phase 2: Consolidation
**November 2026 to January 2027 | ~130 hrs**

Goal: cross from A2 into B1. Output becomes the bottleneck, so speaking hours go up.

**Core**

- **Dreaming Spanish, Intermediate.** 30 min/day minimum.
- **Anki.** Shift to 10 new cards/day, mostly harvested from your own tutor sessions and reading rather than the frequency deck.
- **Grammar clean up.** Work through a reference in short sessions, 20 min twice a week. Target the areas Italian actively misleads you on: ser vs estar, por vs para, subjunctive triggers, and the preterite versus imperfect split.

**Speaking**

- 3 x 60 min/week. Add a third session.
- Introduce a weekly 10 min monologue recording. Pick a topic, speak unscripted, listen back, note the failures, feed them into Anki.

**Reading**

- Olly Richards short stories at A2 to B1, 20 min on Sundays.

**Listening**

- Add Peninsular news audio. RTVE Play and Cadena SER. This is where you train on Barcelona register rather than the Latin American default in most learner media.

**Milestone by end of January**

- ~2500 words
- Can handle a landlord, bank, or utility call with effort
- Following 60 to 70% of a Spain produced TV episode with Spanish subtitles

## Phase 3: Fluency Push
**February to April 2027 | ~130 hrs**

Goal: get output to the point where you are functioning, not translating. Volume shifts hard toward speaking and native material.

**Core**

- **Native media replaces graded media.** Drop Dreaming Spanish for Spain produced series on Netflix, Spanish subtitles only. Never English subtitles.
- **Reading moves to real news.** El País daily, 15 min. Ara for Catalan exposure by osmosis.
- **Anki.** Maintenance only, 5 new cards/day, all self harvested.

**Speaking**

- 4 x 60 min/week. This is the phase where hours in the mouth matter more than anything else.
- Shift at least one session to task based practice: rehearse the actual conversations you will have. Apartment viewings, empadronamiento, NIE appointments, pediatrician visits, school enrollment.

**Denver in person**

- Add one intercambio or conversation group per month if you can find an active one. Meetup and the Mexican Cultural Center are the usual entry points. I do not have current information on which Denver groups are actually running, so verify before building it into the schedule.

**Catalan, light touch**

- April only. 15 min/day of passive exposure. TV3 in the background, a Catalan podcast on a walk. No study, no flashcards. You are training your ear to stop hearing it as broken Spanish, nothing more.

**Milestone by end of April**

- Comfortable B1, B2 in listening and reading
- Can conduct a full unstructured conversation without switching to English
- Handling logistics conversations independently

## Phase 4: Arrival
**May 2027**

- Drop formal study to 3 hrs/week. Your input volume goes up 10x automatically just by being there.
- Keep two tutor sessions/week for the first month. The gap between tutor Spanish and street Spanish is real and a tutor helps you process it.
- Enroll in Catalan through the Consorci per a la Normalització Lingüística. Classes are free or heavily subsidized for residents.
- Default to Spanish in public, accept Catalan when it comes at you, respond in Spanish. Nobody in Barcelona will hold that against you in year one.

## Resource Stack

**Tutoring**

- italki, primary. Filter for tutors from Catalonia, Valencia, or Aragon. $10 to $20/hr for community tutors.
- Preply, backup. Slightly pricier, better scheduling.

**Structured**

- Language Transfer, Complete Spanish. Free, audio only, 90 tracks.
- Dreaming Spanish. Comprehensible input, graded, large Spain based library.

**Vocabulary**

- Anki. Spanish frequency deck plus self built sentence cards.

**Listening**

- RTVE Play, Cadena SER for Peninsular
- Radio Ambulante, El Hilo for narrative Spanish, Latin American accents
- Netflix Spain produced series, Spanish subtitles

**Reading**

- Olly Richards short story collections, A2 to B1
- El País
- Ara, for Catalan exposure

**Catalan, Phase 4 onward**

- Consorci per a la Normalització Lingüística
- Parla.cat
- TV3

## Rules That Make Or Break This

1. Anki daily, even at 5 minutes. Missed days compound worse than missed tutor sessions.
2. Never English subtitles. Spanish subtitles or nothing.
3. Speaking starts in month one and never drops below 2 sessions/week.
4. Do not touch Catalan before April 2027.
5. Peninsular audio from Phase 2 onward. Latin American input is fine for volume but Barcelona is the target.
