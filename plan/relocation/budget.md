## Relocation costs (chat 3, checked 2026-04-30)

- Total visa and relocation services: 10,000 to 25,000 USD depending on visa type and city, excluding household shipping and housing deposits.
- Apostille turnaround: 4 to 8 weeks.
- Housing: landlords often ask 2 to 3 months deposit plus first month plus agency fee. Plan a 1 to 3 month temporary rental on arrival.

## Cost of living (chat 16, checked 2026-04-27)

- Denver roughly 50 to 54 percent more expensive than Madrid or Barcelona overall. Madrid and Barcelona roughly equal to each other.
- A 4,800 EUR per month standard of living in Barcelona or Madrid is about 7,300 USD in Denver (Numbeo).
