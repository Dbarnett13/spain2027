# Daily template

The agent generates plan/daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md from this template, plan/deadlines.md, and the current month in plan/monthly-plan.md.

## Every day

- Anki, 15 minutes, first thing.
- Stacked audio, 30 minutes (Language Transfer in Phase 1, podcasts after).
- Evening block, 45 minutes: tutor session, structured study, or reading, per the current Spanish phase.
- Log any client deposit received in plan/workstreams/income-continuity.md.

## Weekly

- Monday: review deadlines due within 14 days; move any at-risk item to the top of the day.
- Friday: update statuses in tracker.md, deadlines.md, and the monthly plan.
- Saturday: 2 hours Spanish (long tutor session plus one TV episode with Spanish subtitles).
- Sunday: 1 hour Spanish (reading and Anki catch-up).
