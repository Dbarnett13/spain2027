## Public and concertada system (Catalonia)

- I3 to I5 is free in public and concertada schools. Same preinscripcio process for both. Fully online at preinscripcio.gencat.cat.
- 2026-27 calendar: applications March 4 to 18, 2026; provisional lists April 15; final admitted list June 10; matricula June 22 to 30. Late applications lose all priority points. 2027-28 dates not yet published; assume early March 2027.
- Whether the family can file the March 2027 preinscripcio from abroad, before empadronamiento, is not verified. This decides whether the daughter can have a September 2027 public place or must use out-of-period placement after the May arrival.
- Out-of-period placement (matricula viva) runs through the Consorci d'Educacio de Barcelona, Placa d'Urquinaona 6, tel 93 551 10 00. 4,000 to 6,000 pupils a year enter this way. A place is lost if not enrolled within two weeks of assignment.
- Language: Catalan is the vehicular language. Newcomer support classes (aula d'acollida) exist; whether they operate at infantil level is not verified.
- Documents: birth certificate or family book, vaccination record or medical certificate, empadronamiento for proximity points.
