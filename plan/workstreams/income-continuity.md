## What the visa evidence needs (from chat 1 and chat 5, checked 2026-08-07 and 2026-06-08)

- Main applicant alone proves roughly 4,275 EUR per month gross (2026 basis; the 2027 SMI figure will be higher). Target 5,400 to 5,900 USD per month with exchange buffer.
- Income must come from qualifying remote work for clients outside Spain. At least 80 percent of revenue from outside Spain. At least one company client, not only individuals.
- Evidence window is the 3 to 6 months before filing. For an April 2027 filing that is October 2026 through March 2027. Consistency across the window matters more than the average.
- Documents: client contracts, invoices, bank statements physically stamped by the bank (digital PDFs are often rejected by the UGE), matching flow of funds between invoices and deposits. Contracts should show the relationship continues past the move.
- The entity (Superstoke) must have operated at least one year. Since January 2026, self-employed applicants also need apostilled home-country self-employment proof (Schedule C and SE, or IRS transcript).
- Renewal re-proves income over the 3 to 6 months before renewal, at the SMI rate in effect then.

## Constraint on Barcelona partner work

Work for Spanish entities counts against the 20 percent cap on Spanish-source revenue for a self-employed DNV holder (chat 1). Salesforce partner contracts with Spanish entities need to stay inside that cap, or be structured through a non-Spanish entity. Confirm with the immigration lawyer.
